# Making a carousel — a 60-second guide

You don't need design skills or an API key. Here's the whole flow.

## The fast path

1. **Type your topic.** A sentence is plenty: "iPhone 17 Pro vs Galaxy S26 Ultra:
   which camera wins", or "5 sleep myths that cost you rest".
2. **Tap ✦ Make it great.** The Art Director reads your topic and picks the
   template, colours, length, and a writer voice that fits, then writes the deck.
3. **Review & tweak.** Swipe through. Tap **Edit** to change any slide's words,
   **Style** to switch template or colours, **Caption** for the post text.
4. **Export.** **Share all slides** (on a phone) drops them straight into the
   share sheet, or **Download all** saves a zip. Post.

That's it. Every slide is drawn from type and vectors — no AI images — so it's
fast, free to run, and yours to use.

## If you want more control

- **My picks** — generate with the exact template, voice and options you chose,
  instead of letting the Art Director decide.
- **Template** — the rail shows featured looks; **All 12** opens the full set,
  filterable, with colour options per template.
- **Voice** — pick how the copy talks: Straight Shooter, Mentor, Contrarian,
  Analyst, or Hype. Each has a real point of view.
- **Options** — audience, tone, slide count, and research source. Turn research
  on for real, sourced facts (free, keyless). Paste a transcript or notes under
  *Source material* to write from your own content. Add your own API key for
  live web research and AI-written copy.
- **Match a look** (Pro) — upload a screenshot of a carousel you admire; the app
  reads its palette and layout and matches the closest template.

## Honesty, by design

The writer never invents statistics. If it couldn't find a real number, it uses a
label ("BIGGEST FACTOR") instead of a fake percentage, and every figure it does
use is sourced in the caption. When you're on the free tier without a key, decks
are drafted from public sources (Wikipedia / web) and marked as drafts — tighten
the wording and add your own angle before you post.

## Free vs Pro

Free gives you all 12 templates, all 5 voices, the Art Director, keyless
research, and 3 full-resolution, unwatermarked carousels a day. Pro removes the
daily cap and adds Inspiration (match any look), a saved Brand Kit, deeper
research without a key, and drops the caption credit line. See [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md).
